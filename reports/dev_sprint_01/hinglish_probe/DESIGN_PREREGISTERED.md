Forty distinct framing/ordering patterns, with family-specific context/actions and four independently phrased benign/attack intentions. Latin-script light/medium/heavy Hinglish; clean, texting abbreviations, missing vowels, mixed case and typos. Rotate short(5–15words), medium(16–30) and long(31+) phrasing; report actual bins rather than silently counting intended lengths. Use modest emoji variation and varied bank/telco/courier/utility/government identities. SMS metadata never gets prepended to text.
